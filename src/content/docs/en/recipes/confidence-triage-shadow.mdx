## Scenario and input

Three synthetic records: a relevant update, unrelated chatter and a message containing nonpublic customer information, with human labels saved first.

## 1. Fix questions and labels

Define information type, relevance, public risk and proposed save, ignore or review actions with explicit labels.

## 2. Prepare redacted fixtures

Start with synthetic or redacted material rather than sending nonpublic customer content to an external classifier.

## 3. Log without acting

Shadow mode records predictions and reasons without deleting, forwarding or replying; low confidence or invalid structure goes to review.

## 4. Compare with human labels

Count mistaken ignores, mistaken recommendations and missed risks separately, with sample size and source mix.

## 5. Enable bounded routing

Consider automation only for verified, reversible low-risk actions, retaining specific approval for posting, payments, deletion and customer replies.

## A synthetic acceptance fixture

triage.csv: item_id, information_kind, topic_match, public_risk, next_action, confidence, human_label, disagreement. Confidence is not observed accuracy.
