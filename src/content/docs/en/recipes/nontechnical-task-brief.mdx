Translate recruiting, sales, marketing, procurement and design requests into evidence, deliverables and review points.

The source describes several role-specific workflows. This exercise turns them into task briefs without claiming live ATS, advertising or design access.

## Scenario and input

Synthetic request: turn webinar questions into sales follow-up drafts and review a vendor renewal sheet.

## 1. Choose one role outcome

Pick one output: a public recruiting research table, customer brief, question-routing proposal, purchasing comparison or design specification.

## 2. Bound inputs and decisions

Specify job-related criteria, customer lists, budget definitions or design files. Leave missing evidence blank and avoid sensitive personal attributes.

## 3. Preserve evidence and calculations

Attach sources, dates and calculation definitions. Compare advertising data over the same period and seats against a consistent activity inventory.

## 4. Draft reviewable outputs

Prepare separate drafts for messages, budget proposals and design changes, each with an explicit target and action state.

## 5. Validate a sample before expanding

Inspect one candidate, customer or component before expanding. Keep external messages, purchases, campaign edits and terms at a human checkpoint.

## A synthetic acceptance fixture

brief.md records source, rule, output and approval. Example: 12 paid seats with evidence for 7 active seats support a review request, not automatic cancellation of 5.

## Acceptance and review

The brief identifies its evidence, decision rule, output files and next reviewer.
