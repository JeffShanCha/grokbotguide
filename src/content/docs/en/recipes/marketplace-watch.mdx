**The Bot searches Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist or any marketplace you are signed in to on a schedule, and tells you about matching listings with its take on the price.**

## What you get

<RecipeShot slug="marketplace-watch" />

## When to use it

- You want a particular secondhand item but do not want to check every day
- You use these marketplaces regularly

## Before you start

- For sites that need a login (such as Facebook), sign in once in the Bot’s browser using Take over.

## Steps

1. Send the Bot your criteria and run one search by hand to check the results.
2. Adjust, then create a routine that runs twice a day.

## Prompt

Replace the parts in angle brackets with your own.

<CopyTemplate id="marketplace-watch">

```text
Watch for a used <espresso machine>:
- Sites: Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist (<city>)
- Criteria: brand <A / B>, at most <$300>, condition described as good or better, within <30> miles
Search at 9:00 and 18:00 every day and send me new matches: title, price, distance, link and your take (good deal or not, what to ask the seller).
Do not resend listings you already sent. Do not contact sellers, make offers or pay.
```

</CopyTemplate>

## Approvals and permissions

- You contact sellers, make offers and pay.



## Notes and pitfalls

- This is a real workflow from the author of the official Grok Bot 101 guide: watching for an espresso machine, checking with a Chief of Staff Bot for a judgment call, and hunting for a rare plant on an already signed-in Facebook.
- Some sites may not load or may keep asking for verification from a cloud computer; try it for a few days first.
- Do not run it too often; every run uses usage.
