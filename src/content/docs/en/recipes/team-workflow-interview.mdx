## Scenario and input

Synthetic request: review ten public sources weekly, choose three topics and prepare one draft for manual publication.

## 1. Interview for constraints

Ask about audience, platforms, time budget, sources, storage and human decisions, resolving one gap at a time.

## 2. Define collection rules

Specify keywords, time windows, identity keys and reading scope. Record unavailable data without bypassing platform restrictions.

## 3. Agree on three handoffs

Define fields, owners and states for each handoff. Drafting uses approved topics and does not invent source material.

## 4. Compare small roster options

Compare one Bot, two roles and a staged team by maintenance and waiting costs, then choose the smallest workable plan.

## 5. Validate one sample

Walk one source through selection, drafting and fact review, checking the evidence chain before creating roles or schedules.

## A synthetic acceptance fixture

Three handoffs: sources.csv for evidence, topics.csv for review, and drafts/ for approved topics. A topic without approval_id stays pending.
