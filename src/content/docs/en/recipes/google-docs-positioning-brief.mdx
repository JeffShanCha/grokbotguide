**One Bot reads your site and competitors’ sites for gaps; another turns the findings into a positioning brief in Google Docs. You comment in the doc and the Bot revises.**

## What you get

<RecipeShot slug="google-docs-positioning-brief" />

## When to use it

- Writing positioning and site copy before a launch
- Finding a selling point competitors do not own
- You prefer editing in a doc to pasting back and forth in chat

## Before you start

- A Google account plugin. Google Docs is in the Help Center’s sign-in list.
- Two Bots: a Market Researcher that reads sites and a Product Marketer that writes the brief, the split used in the official marketing guide.

## Steps

1. Have the Market Researcher read your site and competitor product pages in its own browser and list the gaps.
2. The Product Marketer takes that context and drafts a positioning brief in Google Docs: one-liner, value points, messaging per channel.
3. Comment in the doc and ask the Bot to revise the same doc.
4. Once positioning is settled, have it outline the landing page and draft search ad variants for testing.

## Prompt

Replace the parts in angle brackets with your own.

<CopyTemplate id="google-docs-positioning-brief">

```text
Read our site <https://...> and these three competitors’ product pages: <A> <B> <C>.
Find what they all emphasize that we have not claimed yet, and list 3 differences we could own.
Then create a Google Doc "<Product> positioning brief": a one-liner, 3 value points, and how to say it on the site, X and LinkedIn.
Send me the link when done. I will comment in the doc; revise the same doc from my comments instead of creating new ones.
```

</CopyTemplate>

## Approvals and permissions

- Start with doc editing only. Grant ads write access or production site access after it has delivered a few times, as the official guide suggests.
- People approve spend and live account changes.



## Notes and pitfalls

- Give each Bot a narrow job; generalist chats get bloated, per the official marketing guide.
- Connect only the tools each job needs.
- Ask it to revise the existing doc rather than creating copies, as the official Files docs recommend.
