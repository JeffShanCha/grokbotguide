**For a ticketing tool without a plugin, the Bot signs in to the agent dashboard in its cloud browser and works tickets like an agent would: labels them, drafts replies and hands bugs to a repro Bot.**

## What you get

<RecipeShot slug="zendesk-support-queue" />

## When to use it

- Tickets keep growing and most answers are already in your help center
- You want the Bot to sort and draft so people handle the hard ones

## Before you start

- An agent account for Zendesk or your ticketing tool, ideally a separate restricted one.
- Links to your help center docs.
- Optional: the Bug Reproduction card from the Bot library.

## Steps

1. Ask the Bot to open Zendesk. When it asks for a login, choose Take over, sign in yourself and choose I’m done.
2. The session stays on the cloud computer, so you do not sign in every time.
3. Start with 10 tickets and compare with your own calls before giving it more.

## Prompt

Replace the parts in angle brackets with your own.

<CopyTemplate id="zendesk-support-queue">

```text
Open the Zendesk agent dashboard (stop and let me take over if it needs a login) and work the Unassigned queue:
1. Label each ticket: <Billing> <Bug> <Feature request> <Account> <Other>
2. Where the help center answers it, draft a reply with links to the docs you used; do not submit
3. For bugs, collect reproduction clues and hand them to "Bug Reproduction"
4. For refunds, cancellations and key accounts, do not draft; list them for me
Finish with a table: ticket number, label, suggested action, whether a draft is ready.
```

</CopyTemplate>

## Approvals and permissions

- Do not submit replies; you review and send.
- Refunds, cancellations and key accounts always go to a person.

## Notes and pitfalls

- The Grok Bot website uses signing in to Zendesk to work the support queue as its example.
- The session lives on the shared computer, so every Bot on your account can use it; use a restricted account or keep other Bots away from support.
- Some sites expire sessions or ask for verification again; the Bot should stop and tell you rather than try to get around it.
- Where a plugin exists, use it; the official docs say plugins are usually more reliable.
