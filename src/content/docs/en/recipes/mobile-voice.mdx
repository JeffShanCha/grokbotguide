**Away from your desk, share from your iPhone gallery or any app straight to a Bot, start a voice chat to hand off work, and answer questions and approvals from notifications.**

## What you get

<RecipeShot slug="mobile-voice" />

## When to use it

- An idea strikes on your commute and you want to hand it off now
- You snapped an invoice, a whiteboard or a screenshot for the Bot to handle

## Before you start

- An iPhone on iOS 18 or later, or Android 9 or later.
- Notifications on, so you hear about results, questions and approvals.

## Steps

1. Share: in the iPhone share sheet choose Grok Bot, pick a chat, choose Attach, add a note and send.
2. Voice: with an empty composer, choose Start voice chat and talk it through.
3. Drafts: emails or Slack messages the Bot prepares appear as cards with Send Email or Discard.
4. Take over: for a two-factor code, open the computer view, take over and choose I’m done.
5. Routines: check run history on the Bot’s profile and use Active to pause or resume.

## Prompt

Replace the parts in angle brackets with your own.

<CopyTemplate id="mobile-voice">

```text
(voice) Add the invoice photo I just took to the "Invoices" sheet; mark it "check" if the amount is unclear.
(voice) Tomorrow morning’s meeting moved to the afternoon; rerun my meeting briefing.
(voice) List every email from today that needs my reply and read them to me.
```

</CopyTemplate>

## Approvals and permissions

- Check recipients and body before tapping Send Email on your phone.



## Notes and pitfalls

- You cannot edit a routine’s schedule or instruction, or test a routine, on mobile; use the desktop app.
- On Android, the share sheet accepts text only for now.
- Teach a task and some advanced desktop controls are not available on mobile.
