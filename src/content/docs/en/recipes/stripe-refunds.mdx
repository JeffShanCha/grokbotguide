**Give the Bot your refund policy. It checks each request and the matching Stripe payment, recommends approve or deny with a reply draft, and acts only after you approve each one.**

## What you get

<RecipeShot slug="stripe-refunds" />

## When to use it

- You sell subscriptions or digital products and get refund requests every week
- Handling refunds means switching between email and the Stripe dashboard

## Before you start

- Stripe access, through a plugin or by signing in to the dashboard in the Bot’s browser, ideally with a restricted member account.
- Your refund policy.
- The Gmail plugin for your support inbox.

## Steps

1. Send the Bot the policy and prompt, and run it on past requests to compare with your own calls.
2. Add an Ask first rule in Auto Review for any refund action in Stripe.
3. Create a routine for 11:00 daily.

## Prompt

Replace the parts in angle brackets with your own.

<CopyTemplate id="stripe-refunds">

```text
Here is our refund policy: <paste or attach>
Every day at 11:00, check the support inbox for new refund requests:
1. Find the payment in Stripe and check purchase date, amount and whether it was already refunded
2. Recommend approve / deny / needs my judgment under the policy, citing the clause
3. Draft the reply to the customer; for eligible long-time customers you may offer <retention offer> in the draft
4. Send me all recommendations as one table
Do not issue any refund in Stripe or send any email without my approval of each one.
```

</CopyTemplate>

## Approvals and permissions

- Refunds move money and need approval one by one. The official security docs list purchases and transfers among the actions to set boundaries for.
- Approval cards from routines expire after about 10 minutes and the action does not run; ask the Bot to retry.



## Notes and pitfalls

- In the official support guide, the Bot reviews refund requests against policy and replies after your approval, and can offer retention deals to some customers.
- One developer reported using a support inbox plus the Stripe API for routine refunds (source: an early-access example collected by explainx).
