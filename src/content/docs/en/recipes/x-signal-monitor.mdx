## Source supplement: align rules before testing thresholds and failed runs

The article's [aehyok/x-viral-monitor](https://github.com/aehyok/x-viral-monitor/blob/8ad0a750c4f794036c4e946aca655eadf55e100d/README.md) is verified at `8ad0a75`. ### Five operational details

1. **Separate scanning and scheduling.** The [Skill inputs](https://github.com/aehyok/x-viral-monitor/blob/8ad0a750c4f794036c4e946aca655eadf55e100d/skill/SKILL.md#L12-L39) come from the caller: accounts, threshold, lookback, timezone and state path. The [routine](https://github.com/aehyok/x-viral-monitor/blob/8ad0a750c4f794036c4e946aca655eadf55e100d/routine/PROMPT.md#L3-L22) adds an hourly 06:04–23:04 Shanghai window, with 06:04 as a non-alerting baseline. Specify your own parameters and schedule first.
2. **Use actual snapshot intervals.** For a previously observed post, both files divide the view change by elapsed hours and require strictly more than 5,000. A gain of 6,000 over two hours is 3,000 per hour.
3. **Choose an explicit first-observation policy.** [SKILL.md lines 59–65](https://github.com/aehyok/x-viral-monitor/blob/8ad0a750c4f794036c4e946aca655eadf55e100d/skill/SKILL.md#L59-L65) floors post age at one minute; [PROMPT.md lines 18–20](https://github.com/aehyok/x-viral-monitor/blob/8ad0a750c4f794036c4e946aca655eadf55e100d/routine/PROMPT.md#L18-L20) floors it at one hour. A five-minute-old post with 1,000 views becomes 12,000/h or 1,000/h respectively. This site's fixture retains a baseline-only first observation. The source rules are kept distinct and the author's repository was not changed.
4. **Separate read failures from zero hits.** The [0909 failed archive](https://github.com/aehyok/x-viral-monitor/blob/8ad0a750c4f794036c4e946aca655eadf55e100d/data/hourly/2026-10-13/0909.json#L37-L41) records `x_read_unavailable`, zero checked posts and unchanged snapshots. Empty hits do not prove a successful read; retain failed accounts, errors and the last valid observation time.
5. **Version state, delivery and archives together.** Retain delivered IDs, snapshots and per-run results separately. An uncertain notification is not confirmed delivery. The current commit reduced the list to two accounts; old archives retain their historical twelve. Use the run's own configuration when auditing it. Instructions describe Git storage, without a standalone implementation establishing atomic state, notification and push behavior.
