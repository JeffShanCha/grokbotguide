**Finish an article or newsletter and the Bot turns it into posts for X, LinkedIn and Substack Notes, saved as Typefully drafts and scheduled once you approve.**

## When to use it

- You have no energy left to rewrite a long piece for social
- You want one piece of content to go out several times across platforms

## Before you start

- A Typefully account connected to Grok Bot. In The AI Maker’s example, Typefully posted Substack Notes and scheduled LinkedIn.
- Three of your best past posts as a voice reference.

## Steps

1. Send the Bot the article and the reference posts.
2. Review the drafts, edit, then let it schedule.
3. Save it as a skill if you use it often.

## Prompt

Replace the parts in angle brackets with your own.

<CopyTemplate id="typefully-repurpose">

```text
Here is the article I just finished: <link or full text>
Turn it into:
- X: one thread of 5 to 7 posts with a hook up front
- LinkedIn: one post of 150 to 250 words
- Substack Notes: 2 short notes
Keep my voice (see the 3 attached posts) and do not invent numbers that are not in the article.
Save everything as Typefully drafts with this plan: X tomorrow 9:00, LinkedIn the day after at 9:00, the Notes over two days (time zone <America/New_York>).
Send me all drafts for confirmation before scheduling.
```

</CopyTemplate>

## Approvals and permissions

- Drafts first, schedule after you confirm, never publish directly. The official security docs also recommend keeping publishing behind approval.

## Notes and pitfalls

- In The AI Maker’s live session, a Substack Note went out and a LinkedIn draft was scheduled for the next day.
- Typefully does not cover every platform; check yours before relying on it.
