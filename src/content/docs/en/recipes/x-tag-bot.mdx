**When a post or thread on X needs work, reply with "@bot" and a request. Your main Bot picks it up on its cloud computer, and the results stay in the Grok Bot app rather than on X.**

## When to use it

- A long thread you want boiled down
- A new tool you want priced and reviewed
- You do not want to switch apps and paste links

## Before you start

- On grok.com, go to Settings → Account and choose Connect next to X Account.
- Get Grok Bot access on the same Grok account and create at least one Bot of your own. Bots shared with you cannot take tagged requests.
- Optional: add the X for Grok Bot plugin so your Bot can search posts and read timelines, mentions, trends and bookmarks. @bot works without it.

## Steps

1. Reply to the post, tag @bot and write the request.
2. @bot replies on X to confirm your main Bot has it.
3. Open the Grok Bot app to follow the work. The first time, X tells you tagged requests start automatically and use your Grok Bot usage.

## Prompt

Replace the parts in angle brackets with your own.

<CopyTemplate id="x-tag-bot">

```text
@bot summarize this thread in 5 key points and send them to my Slack <#reading>
@bot is this tool worth trying? Check its pricing page and user reviews and give me a comparison table
@bot open every link in this post and write a one-line summary of each
```

## Approvals and permissions

- @bot only posts a confirmation on X; the results are not posted publicly.
- Tagged requests start on their own, so state limits in the request, such as "summarize only, do not reply to anyone".

## Notes and pitfalls

- Regions: tagging is not yet available for X accounts based in the EU, the UK, Australia, Iceland, Liechtenstein or Norway. It uses the country X has on record; your location and VPN make no difference. Accounts listed only under a broad region or with no country are not supported yet.
- Posts with a video or GIF in the post, the post it replies to or a quoted post do not go through, and neither do posts that also tag @grok.
- Replying in a thread that already tags @bot does not count unless you tag @bot yourself.
- @bot does not respond once your weekly usage is used up.
