## Scenario and input

Synthetic topic: summarize an official feature announcement into one draft, without publishing it.

## 1. Write the editorial profile

Put audience, columns, examples, tone and forbidden phrases in voice.md; style guidance is not factual evidence.

## 2. Collect evidence before topics

Research records actual reading scope; planning proposes three evidence-based topics for a human to select.

## 3. Draft the approved brief

The writer uses the approved brief, sources and voice.md, leaving personal experience and missing facts for the author.

## 4. Create a separate fact review

Map numbers, quotations, dates and inferences to evidence, removing or marking unverified claims before final review.

## 5. Review after manual publication

The owner publishes. Retrospectives use authorized public data with period and sample size, keeping conclusions weak for small samples.

## A synthetic acceptance fixture

sources.csv → brief.md → draft.md → fact-review.csv. Every factual claim has a source_id and drafting waits for topic approval.
