**Every day the Bot scans the YouTube channels you follow and logs new long-form videos in Notion with title, thumbnail, link and views against the channel’s baseline, so outliers stand out.**

## When to use it

- You make video content and want to know which topics work for peers
- You want a swipe file of standout titles and thumbnails

## Before you start

- A list of channels to watch.
- The Notion plugin and a database.
- Optional: The AI Maker used the Supadata plugin for YouTube data, which is steadier than reading pages.

## Steps

1. Send the Bot the channel list and the prompt below; run it once.
2. Check the view counts and the baseline math.
3. Create a routine for 10:00 daily.

## Prompt

Replace the parts in angle brackets with your own.

<CopyTemplate id="youtube-competitor-watch">

```text
Every day at 10 am, check these YouTube channels for long-form videos (not Shorts) published in the last 24 hours:
<channel 1 link>
<channel 2 link>
For each new video, add a row to the Notion database "<Competitor videos>": channel, title, thumbnail link, video link, published time, current views, the channel’s average views over its last 10 videos, and whether it is clearly above average.
For clear outliers, add two sentences on what the title and thumbnail got right.
If there are no new videos, add nothing and do not notify me.
```

</CopyTemplate>

## Approvals and permissions

- Read-only on YouTube; only add rows to this one database.

## Notes and pitfalls

- This is one of Prompt Warrior’s daily routines.
- Views on new videos are still climbing; compare against the average again a day later (our suggestion).
- Reading pages directly can get rate limited; use a data plugin when you have one.
