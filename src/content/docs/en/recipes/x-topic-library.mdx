## Scenario and input

Three synthetic records: one with metrics, one without metrics and one outside the editorial scope.

## 1. Write the editorial profile

Specify field, audience, goal, tone, excluded topics and available time; benchmark accounts provide observations rather than answers to copy.

## 2. Preserve source provenance

Record permanent links, publication and observation times, and data access method. An unread source is not ready for drafting.

## 3. Separate metrics from interpretation

Keep visible metrics in their original definitions and support interpretations about reach, motivation and audience fit with evidence.

## 4. Develop an original angle

Develop a question and structure for the intended audience, keeping short summaries and linking the original text and cover.

## 5. Review before drafting and retrospectives

Move from pending to approved to draft, then review outcomes with a clear period, sample and confounding factors.

## A synthetic acceptance fixture

topics.csv: source_url, published_at, observed_at, visible_metrics, audience_fit, original_angle, status. Missing metrics are unavailable, not zero; inferences are labeled.
