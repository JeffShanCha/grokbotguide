**The Bot reads who you follow on X and, using public profile information only, builds a Notion board with avatars, bios and profile links, ready to skim before a trip or an event.**

## When to use it

- You follow hundreds of people and cannot remember who does what
- You are visiting a city and want to meet people you know there
- You want to find collaborators by topic

## Before you start

- The Notion plugin, which can query databases and create pages. Create an empty page first.
- The X for Grok Bot plugin, to read your following list.

## Steps

1. Send the Bot the prompt below and start with the first 50.
2. Check the fields and tags, adjust, then do the rest.
3. Optional: a monthly routine to refresh bios and add new follows.

## Prompt

Replace the parts in angle brackets with your own.

<CopyTemplate id="notion-personal-crm">

```text
Turn the people I follow on X into a private personal CRM in Notion, using only public profile information:
- Under the Notion page "<People>", create a database with: name, X handle, avatar, one-line bio, city (only if public), main field, personal website
- Tag by field: <AI coding> <Global SaaS> <Indie hacking> <Investing> <Other>
- Do the first 50, show me, and do the rest after I confirm the format
Do not follow, unfollow, DM or like anyone. Do not search other sites for information about these people.
```

</CopyTemplate>

## Approvals and permissions

- Read-only on X; in Notion, only create under the page you name.
- Use only what people show publicly on their X profile.

## Notes and pitfalls

- This is a real workflow from the author of the official Grok Bot 101 guide, who uses it to reconnect with people while traveling.
- Do not let it dig for personal information on other sites; it is impolite and may break platform rules.
- Work in batches so one run does not use up a week of usage.
