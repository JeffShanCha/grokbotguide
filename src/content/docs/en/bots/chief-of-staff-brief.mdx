## Scenario and input

Synthetic tasks: T1 claims completion without an artifact, T2 awaits review, and T3 has no owner.

## 1. Standardize intake

Record outcome, sources, deadline and permitted actions, with centralized clarification of missing fields.

## 2. Create the ledger

Assign stable identities, one current owner, state and artifact path; a chat claim does not replace evidence.

## 3. Summarize from artifacts

Attach artifacts and acceptance evidence to completed work and actionable explanations to pending or blocked work.

## 4. Control expansion and external actions

Propose a role or handoff for unowned work before creating Bots or performing external actions.

## 5. Produce the concise brief

Report completed, review, blocked and next actions without repeated unchanged updates; confirm timing before scheduling.

## A synthetic acceptance fixture

tasks.csv: task_id, owner, status, source, artifact, next_action. T1 needs verification, T2 needs review, and T3 needs assignment.
