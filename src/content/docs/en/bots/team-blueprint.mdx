## Scenario and input

Synthetic goal: one weekly content brief with no established collection workflow.

## 1. Narrow the outcome

Choose one near-term deliverable with an example of success, a deadline and a bounded scope.

## 2. Draft roles first

Give each role one main artifact, explicit inputs, stop conditions and a recipient.

## 3. Inspect teaching templates

Inspect what a template contains, including external apps and triggers, before treating it as runnable configuration.

## 4. Check apps by task

List necessary connectors, identities and read tests. A recommendation does not establish availability or authorization.

## 5. Expand after approval

Complete one manual brief before saving a template or adding specialists. Separate planning from actual creation and scheduling.
