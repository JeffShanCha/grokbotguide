**Grok Bot is xAI's "AI teammate" product.** Each Bot has a name, a job and long-term memory. It works on a cloud computer that belongs to you, signs in to websites and apps, and carries a task through to the end, coming back only when it needs your approval. It launched in beta on August 11, 2026.

## How it differs from a chat assistant

| | Chat assistant | Grok Bot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it does | Answers questions, writes text | Operates websites and apps to finish tasks |
| Where it runs | Only while you have the chat open | On a cloud computer, even after you close your laptop |
| Memory | Mostly per conversation | Each Bot keeps its role, preferences and summaries of past work |
| Automation | Little or none | Skills (the method) + routines (scheduled or event-triggered) |
| Collaboration | One assistant | Several Bots working in parallel and handing off work |

## Core concepts

- **Bot**: an AI teammate with a clear job, such as Sales Outbound, Expense Manager or Bug Reproduction. The official advice is one focused job per Bot rather than one general helper.
- **Cloud computer**: all your Bots share one persistent cloud computer with a browser, file system and terminal. Files and browser sign-ins are visible to all your Bots, so separate Bots are **not** a security boundary.
- **Connectors and computer use**: use an official connector when one exists (more reliable); otherwise the Bot operates the web page like a person would.
- **Skills**: reusable instructions that spell out inputs, steps, validation, output and what needs approval.
- **Routines**: tell one Bot to run a workflow on a schedule or after an event.
- **Approvals**: sensitive actions such as sending, publishing, paying, deleting or changing production can require you to click Allow.

## Where you use it

Desktop apps for macOS, Windows and Linux; mobile apps for iPhone/iPad and Android (official FAQ). Bots and conversations sync across signed-in devices.
